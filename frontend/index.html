        Guna Milan tables vary between lineages and between commercial software. Kootas with unambiguous classical tables (Nadi, Bhakoot, Gana, Varna, Tara) are exact here; the Yoni koota's intermediate gradations are simplified, so small per-koota differences against other sites are expected.
